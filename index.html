Questions

Good to know.

Does Corvane replace my dispatchers?

No. It takes the repetitive night and weekend work, like chasing updates and fixing routine problems. Your team decides the rules and handles anything unusual.

Which systems does it work with?

Corvane connects to your TMS, your ELD and telematics, email, phone and team chat. Setup takes about two weeks and needs no new software for your team.

What happens when it is not sure?

It asks. Anything outside your rules goes to the right person with the facts already gathered, so the decision takes seconds.

Can we see everything it did?

Yes. Every action is logged with a time, a reason and who approved it, and you can replay any night in full.

Do we need engineers to set it up?

No. Connections are read-only to start, and our team does the setup with yours over four weeks.

Where is our data kept?

In your region, encrypted at rest and in transit. It is never used to train shared models.
